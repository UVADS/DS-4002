  The data is from asking UVA students whethere a hotdog is a sandwich or not. 

## Data Dictionary:

## Quantification of Uncertainty:
 - My sample size is 3 people only
 - The study itself is only following yes or no, which doesn't include any nuance and pigeonholes people who may be in the middle 

## Conclusions:
 The sample size of 3 is simply extremely insufficient to provide a concrete solution. The data itself shows that 2 people said that a hotdog is not a sandwich
