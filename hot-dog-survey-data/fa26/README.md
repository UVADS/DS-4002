## Provenance:
  This data comes from asking UVA students the question "Is a hot dog a sandwich?" and recording their responses. Each of us asked students individually. 

## Data Dictionary: 
  Asked verbally the question to students as a 'yes/no' question. The only recorded responses were 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Quantification of Uncertainty:
  I have a sample size of 46, which is a bit small to generalize to all of UVA. 
  I also asked clusters of like-minded people, which may have similar responses, thus muddying the data.
  I would estimate the uncertainty to be quite high, perhaps up to 10% MoE due to these factors.
