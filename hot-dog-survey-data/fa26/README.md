**Overview**

A survey of the UVA community asking: "Is a hot dog a sandwich?". Response includes information about name, UVA undergrad status, major. This README documents the data's origin, structure, analysis, and findings.


**Provenance**

Source: Google Form ("DS 4002 Quick Survey For A Class Competition"), self-reported responses.
Collection window: 08/28/26 to 08/30/26
Collection method: Voluntary online survey
Known limitations of provenance: self-selection bias, one test/junk row present, free-text major field with inconsistent formatting.
