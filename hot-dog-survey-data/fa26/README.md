## Provenance:
I asked UVA students the question: "Is a hot dog a sandwich?" with responses for yes or no.

## Data Dictionary:
I created a Google Form and shared it on the Facebook page and Messenger chat for my cultural organization, and I also sent the form to friends by directly messaging them.

## Quantifications of Uncertainty:
The sample size isn't representative of the UVA undergraduate population since I only got 27 students. UVA has close to 20,000 undergraduate students, so this is not even 1% of UVA's undergrad population.

## Conclusions:
Based on my data collection methods, my sample size isn't representative of the UVA undergraduate population. However, from my responses, about 70% of people said a hot dog is not a sandwich, while 30% said a hot dog is a sandwich.
