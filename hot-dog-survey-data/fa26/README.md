# Hotdog Data
### Provenance
The data were collected via a survey of UVA undergraduates which we sent to our friends/acquaintances. Answers were collected through a Google Form. These responses were then downloaded as a csv which was used for analysis.
### Data Dicitonary
Column | Description | Responses
:---: | :---: | :---:
Timestamp | Day and time the survey was filled out | month/day/year hour(24hr clock):minute:second
Undergraduate? | Checks if respondant is an undergraduate student at UVA | yes/no
School of Enrollment | Determines which school respondant is part of | Engineering, Data Science, McIntire, Batten, College of Arts & Sciences, Education, Nursing, Architecture
Is a hotdog a sandwhich? | Asks respondants the aforementioned question | yes/no

### Quantification of Uncertainty
The data collected is not quantitative data, so in some sense there is no uncertainty. The answers are binary, either yes or no. However, there is information behind that yes or no answer which we don't know and thus poses some uncertainty. For example, we do not know what the respodnant considers a sandwhich, thus creating uncertainty that their reasoning behind the answer of "no" aligns with ours. 
### Conclusions
It is difficult to come to any concrete conclusions as our sample size is very small. From the data we have gathered, it appears that the majority of undergraduates believe a hotdog is not a sandwich. We cannot really say anything about the distribution among schools of enrollment as we have extremely limited data on that scale.
